On Sunday, July 11, 2021, people took to the streets in **San Antonio de los Baños**, southwest of Havana. They chanted “Freedom,” “Patria y Vida,” and “We are not afraid.”

<MediaImage
  src="/media/legacy/san-antonio-de-los-banos.png"

  caption="San Antonio de los Baños, where the first widely reported demonstration began."

Within hours, demonstrations were reported across the island. Videos recorded by participants documented crowds in Camagüey, Las Tunas, Ciego de Ávila, Pinar del Río, Havana, Santiago de Cuba, and dozens of other communities.

## What made the day historic

The demonstrations were remarkable for their geographic breadth and for the speed with which participants’ own recordings reached the world. For many Cubans, July 11 broke the assumption that fear and isolation would prevent collective public dissent.

The majority of the demonstrations began peacefully. Cuban law does not permit ordinary citizens to carry firearms; weapons were held by the state, military personnel, and police.
