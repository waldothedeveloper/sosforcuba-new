This site preserves an advocacy archive. For time-sensitive claims, current prisoner status, and legal research, use organizations that publish methodology, dates, source documents, and corrections.

## Primary and intergovernmental sources

- [United Nations Human Rights Office](https://www.ohchr.org/) — reports, statements, and treaty-body material
- [Inter-American Commission on Human Rights](https://www.oas.org/en/iachr/) — precautionary measures, country reporting, and thematic work
- [U.S. Department of State human-rights reports](https://www.state.gov/reports-bureau-of-democracy-human-rights-and-labor/) — annual country reporting
- [U.S. Treasury July 2021 statement](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/jy0298) — official account underlying material cited in the archive

## Independent human-rights reporting

- [Human Rights Watch: Cuba](https://www.hrw.org/americas/cuba)
- [Amnesty International: Cuba](https://www.amnesty.org/en/location/americas/central-america-and-the-caribbean/cuba/)
- [Committee to Protect Journalists](https://cpj.org/americas/cuba/) — cases involving journalists and press freedom
- [PEN International](https://www.pen-international.org/) — freedom of expression and writers at risk

## Political-prisoner and civic monitoring

- [Prisoners Defenders](https://www.prisonersdefenders.org/) — political-prisoner reporting and case documentation
- [Cubalex](https://cubalex.org/) — legal information and human-rights monitoring

## How to evaluate a claim

Before sharing a statistic or individual case:

1. Check the publication and event dates separately.
2. Look for a named author or organization and a stated methodology.
3. Distinguish a firsthand account from independently verified reporting.
4. Confirm whether a prisoner’s legal or custody status has changed.
5. Preserve the original link, screenshot, or document when possible.

<Callout title="A note on links">
External websites can move or remove pages. If a source link breaks, search the publishing organization’s archive by the exact report title and date.
</Callout>
